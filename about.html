Who We Are

A Forward-Thinking,
AI-Powered Platform

ePrescribe is a forward-thinking, AI-powered platform built to streamline and modernize prescription and clinic-management for clinics in Mumbai, Maharashtra, and beyond.

By transitioning from traditional paper prescriptions to a digital workflow, clinics can significantly reduce administrative overhead, improve prescription accuracy, and enhance patient safety.

ePrescribe's intuitive interface makes it easy for doctors and staff to quickly generate, manage, and track prescriptions whether working in-clinic or providing online consultations.

Security and privacy are priorities: patient data is stored securely using cloud hosting with strong encryption and access-controls.

By going paperless, clinics also contribute to environmental sustainability and reduce physical storage needs.
